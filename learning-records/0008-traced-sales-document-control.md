# Traced Sales document category control

The learner can distinguish Sales document type, item category, and schedule-line category by business grain; reconstruct the four-input item-category lookup and two-stage schedule-line-category lookup; and separate category determination from category definition. They can trace `VBAK-AUART`, `VBAP-PSTYV`, and `VBEP-ETTYP` back through master-data inputs and classic configuration landmarks.

## Evidence

The learner completed all ten Lesson 15 conceptual questions and five technical checkpoints. With guided prompts, they correctly diagnosed a wrong item category through `VOV4` and missing requirements transfer through `VBEP-ETTYP`, `VBAP-PSTYV`, `MARC-DISMM`, `VOV5`, and `VOV6`.

## Implications

Future lessons can assume the learner understands configuration lookup versus control behavior. Continue retrieval practice on the four item-category inputs, the blank-MRP fallback, exact transaction/table/result mappings, and the difference between configured movement behavior and an executed PGI posting.
