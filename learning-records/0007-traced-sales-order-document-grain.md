# Traced Sales Order document levels and technical grain

The learner can distinguish Sales Order header, item, and schedule-line business scope; separate ordered demand from dated confirmations and delivery progress; and model the hierarchy as nested RAP compositions with independent master objects as associations. They can also identify the classic `VBAK`/`VBAP`/`VBEP` grains and keys, explain parent-value duplication in one-to-many joins, map the hierarchy to Sales Order API nodes, and diagnose a confirmation problem from item demand through schedule-line results to determination inputs.

## Evidence

The learner completed all ten Lesson 14 retrieval questions and all five technical checkpoints. They corrected two important misconceptions: confirmed quantity does not prove delivery, and inflated header totals after a join result from row multiplication rather than item overrides.

## Implications

Future lessons can assume correct document-grain reasoning and proceed to the control chain of sales document type, item category, and schedule-line category. Continue testing the learner's ability to distinguish stored values, derived summaries, configuration inputs, and downstream posting evidence.
