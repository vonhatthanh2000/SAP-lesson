# Mapped SAP SD enterprise-structure controls

The learner can distinguish Company Code, Sales Organization, Distribution Channel, Division, Sales Area, Distribution Chain, Plant, Shipping Point, Sales Office, and Sales Group by the business question each answers. They understand that independently valid units do not form a valid Sales Area unless their exact combination is configured, and that Plant and Shipping Point assignments permit fulfillment paths without guaranteeing successful delivery.

## Evidence

The learner completed all ten Lesson 12 retrieval questions, correctly reconstructed both organizational formulas, diagnosed the missing `V100/10/SP` Sales Area, and modeled Item as a composition child while treating Customer, Sales Area, and Plant as independent associations.

## Implications

Future SD lessons can assume the learner understands the organizational context of a Sales Order and move into Business Partner, material, partner-function, and customer-material master data. Continue reinforcing the difference between an allowed organizational path and a fully executable transaction.
