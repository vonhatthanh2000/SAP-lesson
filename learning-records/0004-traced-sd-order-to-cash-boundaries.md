# Traced SAP SD order-to-cash document boundaries

The learner can trace the core flow from optional inquiry and quotation through Sales Order, Outbound Delivery, goods issue, billing, and payment. They distinguish commercial intent from physical inventory movement and financial settlement, understand header/item/schedule-line scope, calculate open quantity in a partial fulfillment, and distinguish cross-document SD lineage from lifecycle ownership inside a RAP composition.

## Evidence

The learner answered all ten Lesson 11 retrieval questions, corrected delivery-versus-PGI and billing-versus-payment misconceptions, and correctly calculated an order of 10 as delivered 6, billed 6, and open 4.

## Implications

Future SD lessons can assume the basic document chain and move to enterprise structure and master-data determination. Continue testing posting effects and document ownership rather than transaction-code recall.
