# Traced SAP SD master-data provenance

The learner can distinguish reusable master data from the transactional snapshot stored in a Sales Order. They can explain Business Partner categories, roles, organizational data levels, partner functions, customer-material information records, and why order-specific partner assignments belong to the order while referencing independent Business Partners.

## Evidence

The learner completed all ten Lesson 13 retrieval questions. They correctly explained that existing orders normally retain copied values when master data changes, while new orders use the updated values, and identified customer address handling—including time-dependent addresses—as the notable exception.

## Implications

Future lessons can assume the learner understands where Sales Order data originates and can move into sales-document types, item categories, schedule-line categories, and their control logic. Continue reinforcing field-level source precedence and the distinction between reusable master records and transaction-specific snapshots.
