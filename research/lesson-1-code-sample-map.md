Two deliberate limitations matter in an interview:

- RAP110's `acceptTravel` is a compact state update; it does not demonstrate a robust release invariant or a real approver-role check. Add backend validation/action guards and authorization in the portfolio rather than relying on feature control.
- The client is not the owner of the rule. EML and ABAP Unit invoke the same base BO independently of OData, as the [RAP100 EML test](https://github.com/SAP-samples/abap-platform-rap100/blob/0c84e7e44b7c024fbc129ca6d7401aef1ea1d0b1/src/zrap100_tc_travel_eml_sol.clas.abap#L71-L117) demonstrates.
