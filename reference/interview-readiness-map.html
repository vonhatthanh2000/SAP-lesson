Turn interview prompts into project evidence

The question bank is useful for retrieval, but its supplied answers are not primary sources. This map selects the prompts that serve the RAP mission, assigns them to learning milestones, and makes deferred scope explicit.

How to earn “ready”

Retrieve→Explain runtime→Defend a choice→Show evidence
  1. Answer without opening the bank for 60–90 seconds.
  2. State the concept and the runtime consequence.
  3. Connect it to a decision in the Sales Order project.
  4. Point to code, a test, a trace, or a diagram.
  5. Repeat successfully on a later day before marking it ready.

Use the complete interview bank for prompts. Correct answers against SAP Help and commit-pinned SAP-samples artifacts linked by each lesson.

Supporting and deferred scope

Supporting drills

The remaining CDS, Fiori, and BTP questions are useful breadth drills after the core portfolio works. They should not displace transactional RAP practice.

Separate expansion tracks

Most classic RICEFW (A), all CPI (E), and all PI/PO (G) require different systems, artifacts, and evidence. Covering them deeply would broaden the current mission and needs explicit agreement.

An interview-ready answer includes an honest boundary: “I can explain the concept, but my demonstrated implementation evidence is RAP rather than CPI/PI/PO.” Do not claim hands-on depth from a memorized paragraph.

Ask your teaching agent for a timed interview drill only after the matching lesson's pass condition is met.
