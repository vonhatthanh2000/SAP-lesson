Lesson 13 · 90–105 minutes · SAP SD master data

Trace every Sales Order value back to its source

Learn how Business Partner, Material, partner-function, and customer-material data supply reusable facts to a Sales Order. For each important field, identify its source, organizational scope, and whether it was entered, copied, derived, or overridden.

Prerequisite gate

Begin after you can reconstruct Sales Area and Distribution Chain without notes and can distinguish Plant from Shipping Point. Review the Lesson 12 recap if organizational scope is still unclear.

Tangible win: inspect a proposed Sales Order and explain where its partners, payment terms, internal material, customer material number, sales unit, delivering Plant, and Shipping Point came from—without saying “SAP filled it automatically.”

The core model: a transaction assembled from sources

Reference document+ Master data+ Customizing+ Existing document data+ User input→ Sales Order

SAP analyzes several information sources during document entry. A quotation may supply copied document data; Business Partner, Material, pricing, and customer-material records supply reusable defaults; Customizing defines controls and determination strategies; values already present in the document can become inputs to later determinations; and users can enter or change permitted values. The runtime also controls the sequence in which sources are considered. SAP’s current configuration course summarizes these source classes: Identifying the Source of Data in a Sales Document.

Do not memorize one universal precedence order. The source and priority are field-specific and can depend on document type, Customizing, existing values, and release. Ask: which determination owns this field?

Master data versus transactional data

Master dataSales Order data
Reusable facts about a Business Partner, Material, or customer/material relationship.Facts accepted for one specific business transaction.
Maintained for an organizational scope and reused across documents.Copied, derived, or entered under the document's organizational context.
May change as the real-world party or product changes.Usually preserves the commercial decision made for that transaction.
Independent lifecycle.Part of the Sales Order's lifecycle and document history.

Most later changes to customer master data do not retroactively update an existing Sales Order; the document must be changed explicitly if required. SAP identifies address handling as an exception in its essentials course, including time-dependent address behavior: Maintaining Business Partner Master Data.

Do not confuse three kinds of “role”

ConceptQuestion answeredExamples
BP CategoryWhat fundamental kind of party is this?Person, Organization, Group
BP RoleIn which application context may this BP operate, and which data segments are needed?Customer, Customer (Financial Accounting), Supplier
Partner FunctionWhat responsibility does this partner have in this sales transaction?Sold-to, Ship-to, Bill-to, Payer

A BP Category classifies the party. A BP Role enables a business context and its data. A Partner Function assigns responsibility in a process or document. Calling all three “customer role” hides important differences.

Business Partner data has organizational scope

ScopePurposeTypical examples
General / client-wideIdentity shared across application contexts.Name, core address, contact and control data
Company CodeAccounts Receivable and financial processing for one accounting unit.Reconciliation account, payment and correspondence data
Sales AreaSales, shipping, and billing relationship for one Sales Org/Channel/Division.Sales currency, shipping conditions, payment terms, delivering-Plant proposal, partner relationships

A Business Partner can exist at the general level yet still be unusable for a particular Sales Area if the relevant customer sales data is missing. Likewise, billing requires the appropriate accounting data for the Payer. In SAP S/4HANA, Business Partner is the central maintenance approach, while customer roles provide the sales and accounting contexts.

The four customer partner functions

Partner functionBusiness responsibilityData emphasis
Sold-to PartyPlaces or owns the commercial order relationship.Sales relationship, commercial defaults
Ship-to PartyReceives the goods.Delivery address, unloading point, receiving hours
Bill-to PartyReceives the invoice.Invoice address and communication
PayerIs responsible for settling the receivable.Payment-related and financial data

One Business Partner may perform all four functions. A more complex customer can use different partners: headquarters orders, a warehouse receives, an accounts-payable office receives the invoice, and a treasury entity pays. Partner relationships maintained for the Sold-to Party are normally proposed into the Sales Order; the partner determination procedure controls which functions are allowed, mandatory, changeable, or determined at header/item level. See SAP’s Partner Function Concept.

Material master: one product, scoped views

The Material—or Product—master is the central repository for products a company procures, produces, stores, or sells. Departments use different views and organizational levels rather than duplicating the product as unrelated records. SAP introduces this model in Maintaining Material Master Data.

Scope/viewQuestionExamples
GeneralWhat is this product?Description, base unit, product attributes
Sales Org + Distribution ChannelHow is it sold through this Distribution Chain?Sales-unit and sales-processing data
PlantHow is it planned, supplied, and handled at this location?Availability, MRP, loading or fulfillment-relevant data
Accounting / valuation scopeHow is its stock valued?Valuation class and price-related data

A Material can exist generally but still be unavailable for a Sales Order if the required sales or Plant-specific data has not been extended to the relevant organizational levels.

Customer-material information: the specific intersection

Customer+ Sales Organization+ Distribution Channel+ Material→ Customer-Material Info

A customer-material record stores facts that are true only for a particular customer/product commercial relationship. It can contain the customer's own material number and description, delivery data, and agreed tolerances. During order entry, the customer’s material number can be mapped to the company’s internal Material. SAP documents this purpose in Customer Material Information.

Specificity matters: “this Material is sold in pieces” is a general or sales-scope fact; “Customer C100 calls this Material BW-900 and accepts this delivery tolerance” belongs to their relationship.

What happens during Sales Order creation

  1. The user selects Sales Area V100/20/BI and enters Sold-to C100.
  2. Partner determination proposes Ship-to C110, Bill-to C120, and Payer C130 from the customer relationship.
  3. Sales-area data proposes permitted commercial, shipping, and billing defaults such as payment terms.
  4. The user enters customer material BW-900; the customer-material record resolves internal Material M-BIKE-01.
  5. Material and customer-material data propose description, unit, and relationship-specific values.
  6. The delivering Plant is proposed according to the relevant standard determination and available source data.
  7. The Shipping Point is derived from inputs such as delivering Plant, loading group, shipping condition, and Customizing.
  8. The resulting values become data of this Sales Order. Permitted manual changes affect the transaction, not the master record.

“Likely source” is intentionally cautious. To debug a real field, confirm the document type, configuration, current field value, master-data scopes, and release-specific determination rather than assuming every system uses the same precedence.

Transfer the design to your RAP portfolio

SalesOrderRequest
  ├── composition → Items
  ├── composition → OrderPartners
  │     └── association → BusinessPartner
  ├── association → SoldToBusinessPartner
  ├── association → SalesArea
  └── association → DeliveringPlant

Item
  ├── association → Product
  └── optional association → CustomerProductMapping

The master objects remain independent associations. An OrderPartner row, however, can be a composition child if it represents the partner assignment captured for this specific Request. This separates the reusable Business Partner from the transactional fact “C110 is Ship-to for this order.”

Decide explicitly which values are only references and which are copied snapshots. If the UI always dereferences the current Business Partner address, historical Requests may appear to change when master data changes. Standard SAP has specialized address behavior; do not imitate it accidentally with a simple association.

Your 25-minute source-tracing exercise

  1. Copy the seven-row provenance table onto paper.
  2. For each field, label it entered, copied, proposed, derived, or overridden.
  3. Write the organizational scope of its source.
  4. Change the Ship-to from C110 to another permitted partner. Explain why the customer master did not change.
  5. Change C100's master payment terms after the order exists. Predict what happens to the existing order and to a newly created order.
  6. Remove the customer-material record mentally. Explain which customer-specific convenience disappears while the internal Material can still exist.
  7. Draw the RAP model and separate reusable master objects from order-specific partner assignments.
