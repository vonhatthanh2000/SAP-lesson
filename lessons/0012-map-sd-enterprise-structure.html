Lesson 12 · 75–90 minutes · SAP SD enterprise structure

Decide who may sell what, how, and from where

Turn organizational-unit vocabulary into a transaction map. Given a selling scenario, identify the legal accounting unit, sales area, delivering plant, and shipping point—and reject combinations the enterprise structure does not permit.

Prerequisite gate

Begin after you can trace Sales Order → Outbound Delivery → PGI → Billing → Payment and distinguish SD document flow from RAP composition. Review the Lesson 11 recap if those boundaries are not yet automatic.

Tangible win: for a proposed sale, answer six questions without guessing: Which legal entity records it? Who sells? Through which channel? What product line? Which plant can supply it? Which shipping unit executes the delivery?

Enterprise structure is a control model, not merely an org chart

SAP organizational units do more than describe reporting lines. Their assignments constrain valid transactions and provide dimensions for master data, responsibility, authorization, pricing, fulfillment, reporting, and financial integration. SAP describes organizational units as the system representation of legal and operational units in the enterprise: Identifying Organizational Units in SAP S/4HANA Sales.

Ask what each unit controls, not only what it is called. “Vietnam Sales” is ambiguous until you know whether it is a company code, sales organization, sales office, or merely a label.

The six questions

Business questionOrganizational unitCore meaning
Which independent accounting unit records the result?Company CodeThe legal/accounting context for complete financial statements.
Who is responsible for selling?Sales OrganizationThe central SD selling unit responsible for sales and distribution.
How do goods or services reach the customer?Distribution ChannelThe route-to-market strategy, such as direct, retail, or wholesale.
What product or service family is sold?DivisionA grouping of saleable materials or services.
From which operational location can fulfillment occur?PlantA location that can produce, distribute, or provide goods/services.
Which shipping unit processes the delivery?Shipping PointThe highest-level organizational unit for shipping execution.

The formula to remember

Sales Organization+ Distribution Channel+ Division= Sales Area 
Sales Area = WHO sells + HOW it is sold + WHAT is sold

A sales area is one configured combination of exactly one sales organization, one distribution channel, and one division. It is not a fourth independent branch of the hierarchy. SAP uses this combination to express who may sell what and how; sales employees and sales-area-dependent master data can also be scoped to it.

Sales Organization+ Distribution Channel= Distribution Chain

A distribution chain answers only who + how. It does not contain the division. This distinction matters because delivering plants are assigned to a sales organization/distribution-channel combination. SAP’s current customizing lesson explains that a plant can serve multiple distribution chains and may even belong to a different company code in a cross-company scenario: Setting Up the Enterprise Structure in Sales and Distribution.

Assignment rules worth reasoning about

AssignmentConsequenceReasoning cue
Sales Organization → Company CodeEach sales organization is uniquely assigned to one company code; one company code can have multiple sales organizations.Which legal books receive the sales result?
Distribution Channel ↔ Sales OrganizationA channel must be assigned before that sales organization can use it.Is this route to market allowed for this seller?
Division ↔ Sales OrganizationThe seller can be permitted to handle multiple product/service groups.Is this seller responsible for this product family?
Sales Org + Channel + DivisionThe valid combination forms a Sales Area.Is this exact who/how/what combination configured?
Distribution Chain ↔ PlantThe plant is allowed to deliver for that seller and channel.Can this route to market use stock or service from this location?
Shipping Point ↔ PlantShipping execution is available for that plant.Which dispatch unit can process this delivery?

A plant is not part of the Sales Area formula, and a shipping point is not a synonym for a plant. Each outbound delivery is processed by one shipping point, while a plant can have multiple shipping points. The shipping point used for a transaction is determined from shipping context; detailed determination belongs in a later fulfillment lesson.

Enterprise structure answers whether the organizational path is allowed. It does not by itself guarantee a successful order. Customer/material sales-area data, partner functions, stock, availability, pricing, credit, and other controls can still stop or alter processing.

Where Sales Office and Sales Group fit

A Sales Office commonly represents a regional or physical sales location. A Sales Group represents an area of responsibility or team within a sales office. They support responsibility assignment, analytics, and selection, but neither is part of the Sales Area formula. 

Sales Area = Sales Organization + Distribution Channel + Division

Not included: Sales Office, Sales Group, Plant, Shipping Point

This distinction is confirmed in SAP’s current Sales Organization reference.

Transfer the idea to your RAP portfolio

Organizational references are independently meaningful master or configuration data. They normally should not become composition children of your custom SalesOrderRequest. 

SalesOrderRequest
  ├── composition → Items
  ├── association → Customer / Business Partner
  ├── association → Sales Area reference
  └── association → Delivering Plant reference

A validation can reject a Request whose selected Sales Area is not allowed, but do not invent standard SD determination rules inside the portfolio and claim that they reproduce S/4HANA. Document the custom simplification and keep standard integration behind a released API or explicit adapter.

What a technical failure means

Never repair enterprise structure with direct SQL. Customizing transactions maintain validation, dependent entries, and transport recording. In SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, use the supported configuration activities rather than assuming every classic OV* transaction is available.

Your 20-minute mapping exercise

  1. Draw six boxes labeled Company Code, Sales Organization, Distribution Channel, Division, Plant, and Shipping Point.
  2. Write the question answered by each unit beneath its box.
  3. Circle Sales Organization + Distribution Channel + Division and label the combination “Sales Area.”
  4. Circle Sales Organization + Distribution Channel and label it “Distribution Chain.”
  5. Connect each allowed distribution chain to its plants, then each plant to its shipping points.
  6. Explain why V100/10/SP is invalid even though V100, 10, and SP each exist separately.

Finally, invent one second company code and one cross-company plant scenario. State which assignment would make the fulfillment path possible, but do not assume the complete intercompany process is configured merely because that assignment exists.
